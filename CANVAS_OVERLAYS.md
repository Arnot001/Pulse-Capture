# Position overlays in Quick Edit

Insert or select a timed cue in **EMOJIS** or **GIFS / REACTIONS**, then choose
**POSITION ON VIDEO**. With playback paused, you can also click a visible overlay
directly in the video preview.

- Drag the selected overlay to move it.
- Drag a corner handle to resize it while keeping its aspect ratio.
- Drag the round handle to rotate it.
- Press **Escape** during a drag to restore its starting values.
- Reopen **EMOJIS** or **GIFS / REACTIONS** to see or edit the synchronized numeric
  X, Y, size and rotation values. **UPDATE / REPLACE** applies numeric changes.

Selection follows cue timing after trims, cuts and speed changes. Overlapping
overlays select from the topmost layer. Crop, privacy and motion tools retain
their own canvas controls; choose **FORMAT** to position overlays again.

Positions use the existing normalized output coordinates, including Full Frame
Shorts. The existing renderer handles preview and export, and selection handles
are never included in the rendered video. GIF looping, animations, sound effects,
caption themes and Pulse Edit Plan v2 fields retain their existing behavior.
