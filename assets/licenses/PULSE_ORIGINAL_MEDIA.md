# Pulse Original Media

The reaction GIFs in `assets/reactions`, the emoji drawings in `assets/emoji`,
the sound recipes in `editor/sound_design.py` and `editor/reaction_library.py`,
and the compositions in `editor/music.py` were created for Pulse Capture.
They use original vector drawings, oscillators, noise, envelopes and arrangements.
No third-party recordings, film clips, celebrity likenesses, meme audio clips,
music samples or character artwork are included in this pack.

Permission is granted to use, modify and include this original media in personal
and commercial videos, including monetised videos, without attribution. The
original media and its generators may be redistributed with Pulse Capture.
No warranty or platform-specific claim of copyright clearance is made.

The animal effects are stylised synthetic designs, not wildlife recordings.
The Memes category contains original editorial gestures, not recordings of
recognisable third-party memes.

Text in the GIFs is rasterised using the bundled DejaVu Sans Bold font.
Its separate third-party licence is included as `LICENSE_DEJAVU` in this folder.
The generated media is not a redistribution of font software.

Per-GIF source, licence, duration, frame timing and SHA-256 records are in
`assets/reactions/catalogue.json`. The complete pack inventory and sound recipe
fingerprints are in `assets/media_manifest.json`.

Imported user files are not covered by this permission. YouTube downloads retain
their individual licence conditions and any required attribution. Pulse Capture
stores the user's supplied credit verbatim; it does not infer permission from
a filename or from a generic “royalty-free” label.
