# Pulse Original Music Library

These soundtrack recipes are original to the Pulse project and are generated
locally by `editor/music.py`. They do not contain third-party recordings,
samples, loops, stems, or downloaded music.

## Pulse Music Engine v2

The second-pass engine gives every track a distinct arrangement and synthesis
palette rather than sharing one simple loop recipe. Each built-in now has a
12-bar Intro → A → B → Lift → Turn structure, separate A/B chord progressions,
track-specific bass and lead patterns, chord inversions/extensions, deterministic
velocity/pitch humanisation, drum variation/fills, arpeggios or counter-motifs,
synthesized risers/impacts, stereo movement, light side-chain-style ducking where
appropriate, and soft mastering.

Built-in tracks:

- Pulse // Night — dark tech — 96 BPM
- Pulse // Drive — energetic electronic — 124 BPM
- Pulse // Focus — minimal background — 88 BPM
- Pulse // Lift — upbeat promo — 118 BPM
- Pulse // Horizon — cinematic — 76 BPM
- Pulse // Circuit — clean tech — 110 BPM

The palettes are deliberately different:

- Night: dark pads, glass hook, pulsing low end, restrained tech drums
- Drive: brighter synth stack, active bass, four-on-the-floor energy, fills
- Focus: warmer sparse harmony, softer rhythm, minimal lead activity
- Lift: brighter extended chords, upbeat drums, rising melodic movement
- Horizon: wide cinematic harmony, sparse percussion, swells and impacts
- Circuit: tighter digital tones, syncopated low end, crisp arps/glitch feel

The generated built-in tracks are intended for use in Pulse Capture exports,
including personal and commercial videos, and for redistribution as part of the
Pulse Capture application. They remain instrumental and mixed as background beds
so narration can stay prominent.

Imported MP3/WAV files are not covered by this note. Users remain responsible
for permission to use music they import.
